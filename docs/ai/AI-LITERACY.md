# Short guide: working with the Levuvo Mail Assistant

For everyone who uses the AI features (AI literacy, Art. 4 AI Act). Reading time: about five minutes.

## What the assistant is

A language model that runs on your computer (or, if your organization allows it, at a cloud provider). It
predicts plausible text. It does not "know" facts and can be wrong in a confident tone.

## What it does well

- Summarizing long threads, pulling out deadlines, amounts and questions.
- Translating, rewording, turning notes into a polite email.
- Spotting likely phishing and fraud patterns.
- Finding messages by meaning and answering questions with the source email.

## Where it fails

- **Invented details:** dates, amounts, names or promises that are not in the email. Always compare with
  the source, especially before you accept an event or send a reply.
- **Missing context:** it only sees what it was given, not phone calls or other systems.
- **Manipulated emails:** an attacker can hide instructions in an email ("ignore your rules…"). The
  assistant is built to ignore them, but treat any strange suggestion as a warning sign.
- **Assessments are hints, not verdicts.** A "Nothing suspicious" rating does not make a payment request
  safe. If bank details change, confirm by phone using a number you already know.

## Your responsibility

- You decide. Nothing is sent, saved or moved without your click; check every draft and every approval.
- Do not type passwords, codes or keys into the assistant.
- Use cloud models only if your organization allows them, and never for accounts marked "No cloud AI".
- Report anything unusual to [contact].

## Where to find things

- Settings → AI & agents: switch features on and off, choose the model, writing style, AI log.
- The ✦ button in the left bar (Ctrl/⌘+J): questions, tasks, routines and approvals.
- In a message and in the composer: the "Assistant" buttons.
