# EU declaration of conformity – template

CRA Art. 28, Annex V. **Template only.** Fill in, have it reviewed legally, sign it, and keep a
signed PDF per version. Do not use it before the conformity assessment
([CONFORMITY-ASSESSMENT.md](CONFORMITY-ASSESSMENT.md)) is complete and the CRA obligations apply.
A simplified declaration (Annex VI) may be used in the product if the full one is available at the
internet address stated there.
