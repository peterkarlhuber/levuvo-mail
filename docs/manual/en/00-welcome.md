# Welcome

Levuvo Mail brings email, calendar, contacts, tasks and collaboration together in one app for macOS and Windows. Everything runs on your computer. Your messages and passwords never pass through a Levuvo Mail server.

This manual shows you step by step how to set up the app and use it every day. You don't have to read it from start to finish. Pick the task you want to do from the table of contents.

## How to read this manual

- Words in **bold** are labels you find in the app exactly like that, for example the **New message** button.
- An arrow shows a path through the app: **Settings → Accounts** means "open the settings, then the Accounts section".
- Keys are shown in a box, for example `Ctrl` + `K`. On a Mac, press `⌘` (Command) instead of `Ctrl` and `⌥` (Option) instead of `Alt`.
- Technical terms such as IMAP or OpenPGP are explained briefly where they first appear. You find all of them in the chapter **Terms explained** at the end.

> **Tip:** In the app you find this manual at any time through the question mark in the left bar or under **Settings → Help**. You can search it there. The assistant also answers questions about using the app.
