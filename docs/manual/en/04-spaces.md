# Spaces for collaboration

A **space** gathers everything about a project, client or topic in one place: shared notes, important emails with internal comments, tasks and events with owners, files and a list of recent changes.

Open spaces with `Alt` + `4`.

![A space with overview, notes, tasks and files](spaces.png)

## How spaces work

There is no central server. Every member has their own encrypted copy of the space on their computer. Levuvo Mail sends changes as encrypted emails between the mailboxes. These emails land in the "Levuvo Spaces" folder and are processed automatically. You don't need to open them.

End-to-end encrypted means: only the members can read the content. Not even your email provider can see it.

## Create a space and invite people

1. Click **New space**, enter a name and optionally a description, and click **Create space**.
2. Open the **Members** tab and invite people by email address. Choose their role:
   - **Owner** may do everything, including managing members.
   - **Editor** may create and change content.
   - **Reader** may only read.
3. The invited person needs Levuvo Mail too. They get an email, see the invitation under **Spaces** and click **Join**.

> **Tip:** When someone joins, Levuvo Mail shows both sides a safety code. Compare it quickly on the phone. If it matches, nobody is secretly listening in.

## Work in a space

Switch the tabs at the top with a click or with the arrow keys.

| Tab | What for |
|---|---|
| Overview | What happened recently |
| Chat | Short messages to all members, end-to-end encrypted. Reply, edit or delete your own messages; `Enter` sends, `Shift` + `Enter` starts a new line; **Start video call** sends everyone a Jitsi link |
| Notes | Texts that several people edit at the same time |
| Mails | Emails on the topic with internal comments |
| Tasks | Tasks with owners and due dates |
| Events | Shared events |
| Files | Files with earlier versions |
| Members | Who is in and which role they have; your safety code to compare and copy |
| Settings | Name and description, assistant and cloud AI, sync, danger zone |

Chat messages usually arrive within a few seconds. Levuvo Mail counts unread ones on the left bar, in the list of spaces and on the tab, on this device only: there are no read receipts.

To put an email into a space, open it, click **Add to space** in the **…** menu and choose the space.

The AI assistant only sees a space if its owner allows it in the space's **Settings** tab. Cloud AI needs a separate, additional permission there.

The **Settings** tab (the gear at the far right of the tab bar) is laid out like the app settings: cards for **General**, **Levuvo Mail Assistant in this space** and **Sync**. Everything saves automatically as soon as you leave a field or flip a switch. At the bottom sits the red-framed **Danger zone** with **Hand over space**, **Dissolve space**, **Leave space** or **Delete copy**. Each of these asks first.

Your **safety code** is in the **Safety codes** card of the **Members** tab. It is the fingerprint of your space key, the same in all spaces and not secret. **Copy** puts it on the clipboard, for example to paste into a message.
